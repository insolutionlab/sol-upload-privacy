Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the application operated by Insolution Lab (insolutionlab@gmail.com) · Effective date is November 10, 2026.

This app is used solely by the operator to upload self created music videos and to manage video metadata including titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and channel banners across two YouTube channels (Midnight Drive Studio · Daydream Sound Studio).

Data accessed

This app accesses the YouTube Data API strictly for video uploading and channel management purposes.

This app does not collect, store, or process any personal information belonging to other individuals or third parties.

How data is used

Accessed data and permissions are used exclusively to publish operator created music videos and modify metadata on the two designated channels.

Data is never used for advertising, marketing, or analytical purposes.

This app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including all Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored only on a single local computer belonging to the operator.

Authorization keys are never transferred, exposed, or sold to external entities.

Sharing

Insolution Lab does not share, disclose, or sell any user data or access keys to third parties.

Revoking access

Access permissions can be revoked at any time by visiting https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and deleting access for this app.

Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Insolution Lab at insolutionlab@gmail.com.
